✓ Interpretation Boundary
The universe is a visualization of graph objects, relationships and available telemetry. A visible moving shape does not by itself prove that a physical file transfer or runtime execution occurred. Execution claims require corresponding live telemetry, Veritas evidence or receipts.

How To Read This Universe

1. Nodes are components or governed objects.Single-click a node for its inspector. Double-click or press Enter on expandable nodes to enter that universe.
2. Lines are graph relationships.The line connects source and destination components. Its moving objects show activity or object flow over that relationship.
3. Symbol answers “what is moving?”⊞=scope/directory, □=file/record, Δ=model/skill/job, ⊕=capability/harness, ∴=receipt/evidence, ⌬=database/data, ∑=MCP/tool, ○=generic runtime event.
4. Color answers “who owns it?”Category colors remain consistent with the LeeWay Signal Language legend.
5. Moving geometry is inspectable.Zoom in, click the moving shape, and the right-side inspector identifies the graph object and its route. A file-backed object shows its known source path.
6. Presentation is not proof.The visualization reflects embedded/live graph records; appearance alone does not prove runtime health, execution, or Veritas approval.
